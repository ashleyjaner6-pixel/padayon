Starting Where I Am

Ashley Janer September 22, 2026

College is a new chapter.

It can be exciting, but it can also be confusing. There are days when I feel motivated and ready to do everything, and there are also days when I feel tired, lost, or unsure of what I am doing.

Sometimes, I think I need to have everything figured out. I need to know where my life is going, what career I want, and what kind of person I want to become.

But maybe I don't have to know everything yet.

Maybe I just need to take the next step.

Walking With God

One thing I am learning in this season is that I don't have to walk through college alone.

There are many things I cannot control. I don't know what will happen next year, where my choices will lead me, or what challenges I will face. But I know that God is already there.

Proverbs 3:5-6 says:

“Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.”

Sometimes trusting God is easier to say than to do.

There are moments when I worry about my future or compare myself with other people. I see people who seem to be ahead of me, and I wonder if I am doing enough.

But God reminds me that my journey does not have to look like someone else's.

College Is More Than Grades

Of course, school is important. There are assignments, projects, exams, deadlines, and sometimes sleepless nights.

But I believe college is also a time to grow as a person.

I am learning how to be responsible, how to handle pressure, how to deal with failure, and how to keep going even when things don't go according to plan.

Some lessons don't come from the classroom.

They come from difficult days.

They come from mistakes.

They come from waiting.

And sometimes, they come from simply choosing to trust God again.

One Step at a Time

I don't know exactly where this journey will take me.

And honestly, I don't think I need to know everything right now.

For now, I want to learn, grow, serve God, and become the person He wants me to be.

This blog is a small part of that journey.

I want to share the things I learn along the way—the struggles, little victories, thoughts, Bible lessons, college experiences, and moments when I see God's goodness in ordinary days.

Maybe someday, I'll look back at these posts and see how much I have changed.

For now, I'm simply starting where I am.

One day.

One lesson.

One prayer.

One step at a time.

“This is the day which the LORD hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.” — Psalm 118:24

Let's walk this path together.
