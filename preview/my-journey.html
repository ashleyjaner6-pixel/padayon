My Journey

Ashley Janer September 22, 2026

Still Becoming

I don't have everything figured out.

I'm still learning, still growing, and still trying to understand where God is leading me.

My journey through college hasn't always been easy. There have been exciting moments, difficult days, unexpected changes, and times when I questioned myself and my direction.

But through all of it, one thing remains the same:

God has been with me.

Growing Up and Growing in Faith

There was a time when I thought growing up was mostly about becoming independent.

Going to college, making my own decisions, planning my future, and learning how to handle responsibilities.

But I'm slowly realizing that growing up also means learning when to depend on God.

I don't always have the answers.

Sometimes I worry about my future. Sometimes I make mistakes. Sometimes I lose motivation or feel like I'm not doing enough.

And sometimes, I drift away from God without even noticing.

But every time I turn back, I am reminded that He is still there.

My College Season

College is more than classrooms, assignments, and exams.

For me, it has become a season of discovering who I am and who I want to become.

I'm learning how to handle pressure.

I'm learning from my mistakes.

I'm learning that failure doesn't have to be the end.

I'm learning to be patient with myself.

Most importantly, I'm learning that my future doesn't have to be completely clear for me to keep moving forward.

Proverbs 16:9 says:

“A man's heart deviseth his way: but the LORD directeth his steps.”

I can make plans.

I can have dreams.

I can work hard for the future I want.

But ultimately, I want God to direct my steps.

The Roads I Didn't Expect

Not everything in my life has happened the way I imagined.

Some plans changed.

Some doors closed.

Some things took longer than I wanted.

There were moments when I wondered why God allowed certain things to happen.

I still don't have answers to everything.

But I've learned that a different path doesn't necessarily mean a wrong path.

Sometimes God uses unexpected seasons to teach us things we wouldn't have learned otherwise.

Sometimes the road we didn't plan to take becomes part of the story He is writing.

Where I Am Now

I'm still on the way.

I still have dreams.

I still have things I want to accomplish.

I still have areas where I need to grow.

And I know there will be more challenges ahead.

But I don't want to rush through this season just to reach the next one.

I want to learn from where I am now.

I want to enjoy the little moments.

I want to grow in my faith.

I want to become more disciplined, more grateful, and more willing to trust God.

I want my college years to be more than a preparation for a career.

I want them to be part of my walk with God.

One Step at a Time

I don't know exactly where this journey will lead.

But I know I don't have to see the entire road before taking the next step.

For now, I'll keep learning.

I'll keep praying.

I'll keep trying.

I'll keep growing.

And when I fall, I'll get back up.

Because this isn't about having a perfect journey.

It's about continuing to walk.

“The steps of a good man are ordered by the LORD: and he delighteth in his way.” — Psalm 37:23

This is my walk.

This is my journey.

And I'm still becoming.
