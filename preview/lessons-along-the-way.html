Lessons Along the Way

Ashley Janer September 22, 2026

 

Life has a funny way of teaching us.

Sometimes the lessons come from books and classrooms. Other times, they come from mistakes, disappointments, answered prayers, waiting, and ordinary days.

I'm still learning, but here are some of the lessons God has been teaching me along the way.

1. I Don't Have to Have Everything Figured Out

There was a time when I thought I needed to know exactly what I wanted for my future.

But life doesn't always work that way.

Sometimes, God only shows us the next step.

I may not know where I'll be years from now, but I can still be faithful with what He has given me today.

“Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.” — Psalm 119:105

A lamp doesn't have to light the entire road. Sometimes it only gives enough light for the next step.

2. Failure Doesn't Define Me

I've made mistakes.

I've had moments when I wasn't proud of my decisions. I've procrastinated, lost motivation, and sometimes wondered if I was capable of doing better.

But failure can teach us something.

Instead of letting one mistake convince me that I'm not good enough, I can learn from it and try again.

Proverbs 24:16 says:

“For a just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again.”

Falling isn't the end.

Getting back up matters.

3. Comparison Steals My Peace

It's easy to look at other people's lives and wonder why mine doesn't look the same.

Someone may be ahead in one area while I'm ahead in another.

Someone else's success doesn't mean I've failed.

God gave each of us a different journey.

I don't need to become someone else to have a meaningful life.

I just need to become the person God is calling me to be.

4. Waiting Can Be Part of God's Plan

Waiting is one of the hardest things to do.

We want answers quickly.

We want our prayers answered immediately.

We want to know what comes next.

But sometimes, waiting teaches us patience and trust.

“Wait on the LORD: be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine heart.” — Psalm 27:14

Maybe the waiting isn't wasted time.

Maybe God is preparing something in me before He changes something around me.

5. Small Progress Still Counts

Not every day is productive.

Some days I accomplish a lot.

Other days, just getting through the day feels like enough.

I've learned that growth doesn't always look big.

Sometimes it's choosing to pray instead of worry.

Sometimes it's opening my Bible again after being away from it.

Sometimes it's finishing one task instead of giving up on everything.

Small steps still move us forward.

6. God Is Worth Coming Back To

There have been times when I became distracted.

School, responsibilities, problems, and everyday life can slowly push God to the side.

But whenever I return to prayer and His Word, I'm reminded that I was never meant to walk this life without Him.

Coming back to God doesn't require a perfect life.

It simply requires a willing heart.

Still Learning

These aren't lessons I've completely mastered.

I'm still learning them.

I still struggle.

I still make mistakes.

I still have days when I forget what I've already learned.

But maybe that's what growing is.

Not becoming perfect overnight, but continuing to learn and allowing God to work in us little by little.

My journey isn't finished.

There are still many roads ahead, many lessons to learn, and many prayers to pray.

For now, I'll keep walking.

One lesson at a time.
One prayer at a time.
One step at a time.
