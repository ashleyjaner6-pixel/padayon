Still Walking

Ashley Janer September 22, 2026

When I look back, I realize that my journey has never been perfect.

There were good days.

There were difficult days.

There were moments when I felt close to God and moments when I felt far away.

There were times when I knew exactly what I wanted, and times when I had no idea what I was doing.

But through every season, I kept learning.

I Have Come This Far

Maybe I haven’t reached all the goals I once imagined.

Maybe there are still things I want to change about myself.

Maybe I still have questions about my future.

But I can look back and see that I’ve grown.

I’ve survived days I thought would break me.

I’ve learned from mistakes.

I’ve experienced things I never expected.

And I’ve seen God’s goodness in moments I almost overlooked.

Sometimes we focus so much on where we’re going that we forget to appreciate how far we’ve already come.

My Walk Isn’t Perfect

I used to think having strong faith meant never doubting, never struggling, and always knowing what to do.

I’m learning that faith can also look like getting back up.

It can look like praying after a long period of silence.

It can look like opening God’s Word when you don’t feel like it.

It can look like trusting Him even when you don’t understand.

It can look like saying:

“Lord, I don’t know what I’m doing, but I’m still going to follow You.”

There Is Still More Ahead

This isn’t the end of my journey.

There are still classes to finish.

Dreams to pursue.

Mistakes to learn from.

Prayers to pray.

People to meet.

Places to go.

And many more lessons God may teach me along the way.

I don’t know exactly what the future will look like.

But I don’t want to spend my time worrying about everything I cannot see.

I want to be faithful with what is in front of me today.

Keep Walking

If you’re reading this and you’re also in a season of uncertainty, I hope you remember something:

You don’t have to have your whole life figured out.

You don’t have to be perfect.

You don’t have to compare your journey with someone else’s.

And you don’t have to walk alone.

Take the next step.

Even if it’s small.

Even if you’re scared.

Even if you don’t know where the road will lead.

Trust God with the part you cannot see.

“The LORD shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in from this time forth, and even for evermore.” — Psalm 121:8

Until the Next Chapter

This page may be the end of this part of my story, but it isn’t the end of my walk.

There will be new seasons.

New challenges.

New beginnings.

And probably a few unexpected turns along the way.

But whatever comes next, I want to keep God at the center.

I want to keep learning.

Keep growing.

Keep praying.

Keep trusting.

And keep walking.

One day at a time. One prayer at a time. One step at a time.

Because the journey isn’t about knowing exactly where I’m going.

It’s about knowing Who I’m walking with.

And I’m still walking.
