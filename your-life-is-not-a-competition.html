Your Life Is Not a Competition

Ashley Janer September 22, 2026

Sometimes, all it takes is a few minutes of scrolling.

You see someone traveling.

Someone getting good grades.

Someone starting a business.

Someone getting their dream job.

Someone looking happy and confident.

And suddenly, you start looking at your own life differently.

“Why am I not there yet?”

The Comparison Trap

Social media makes it easy to compare.

We see people’s achievements, but we don’t always see their struggles.

We see the finished product, but not the process behind it.

We see their best moments while we’re experiencing our ordinary days.

That’s why comparison can be dangerous.

We end up comparing our real life to someone else’s highlight reel.

And sometimes, we forget to appreciate what God is already doing in our own lives.

God Gave You Your Own Journey

Everyone has a different path.

Some people graduate early.

Others take longer.

Some know exactly what career they want.

Others are still figuring it out.

Some people seem to have everything together.

Others are quietly struggling.

That doesn’t mean one person’s life is more valuable than another’s.

God didn’t create us to copy each other’s lives.

Galatians 6:4 says:

“But let every man prove his own work, and then shall he have rejoicing in himself alone, and not in another.”

Instead of constantly asking, “Am I ahead of them?”

Maybe I should ask:

“Am I growing from where I was before?”

Celebrate Without Comparing

It’s okay to be happy when someone else succeeds.

Someone else’s blessing doesn’t mean God has forgotten you.

Someone else’s achievement doesn’t take away your own potential.

Their chapter is not your chapter.

Their timing is not your timing.

And their journey is not your journey.

You can celebrate them while continuing to work on yourself.

Be Thankful for Small Progress

Maybe your progress doesn’t look impressive right now.

Maybe you’re just trying to pass your subjects.

Maybe you’re learning how to manage your time.

Maybe you’re becoming more responsible.

Maybe you’re slowly becoming closer to God again.

Those things matter too.

Growth isn’t always dramatic.

Sometimes it’s simply realizing that you’re handling something better than you did before.

Sometimes it’s choosing not to give up.

Sometimes it’s getting back up after failing.

And sometimes, it’s trusting God one more time.

Look Up, Not Around

When I spend too much time looking at everyone else, I lose sight of where I’m going.

So I want to learn to look up instead.

Look toward God.

Ask Him for wisdom.

Ask Him for direction.

Ask Him to help me become the person He wants me to be.

James 1:5 says:

“If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God…”

I don’t need to compete with everyone around me.

I just need to faithfully walk the path God has given me.

So if you’re feeling behind today, take a breath.

Put the phone down for a moment.

Look at how far you’ve already come.

Then take the next step.

Your life is not a competition.

You don’t have to outrun everyone else.

You just have to keep walking with God.
