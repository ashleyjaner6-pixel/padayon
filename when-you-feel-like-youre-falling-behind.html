When You Feel Like You’re Falling Behind

Ashley Janer September 22, 2026

Have you ever looked at someone else’s life and suddenly felt like you’re not doing enough?

Maybe they have better grades. Maybe they already know what they want to do after college. Maybe they seem more confident, more successful, or simply more prepared than you.

And then you start asking yourself:

“Am I falling behind?”

I’ve asked myself that question too.

Everyone Has Their Own Pace

College can sometimes feel like a race.

We see other people achieving things, graduating, getting jobs, building their careers, and reaching goals. It’s easy to compare our progress with theirs.

But the truth is, we don’t all start at the same place.

We don’t have the same struggles, opportunities, responsibilities, or circumstances.

So maybe I don’t need to measure my journey using someone else’s timeline.

God doesn’t ask me to become someone else.

He asks me to faithfully walk with Him where I am.

God Is Not Finished With Me

There are days when I feel like I should be doing more.

I should be studying harder.

I should be more productive.

I should already know what I’m going to do with my future.

But sometimes, I forget that growth takes time.

Philippians 1:6 says:

“Being confident of this very thing, that he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ.”

God is still working.

Even when I don’t see progress.

Even when I make mistakes.

Even when I feel like I’m going backwards.

My current situation isn’t the end of my story.

Don’t Give Up Because You’re Not There Yet

Maybe you’re also in a season where you feel stuck.

Maybe your grades aren’t where you want them to be.

Maybe you’re struggling to stay motivated.

Maybe you’re unsure about your future.

Or maybe you’re simply tired.

It’s okay to admit that.

Being tired doesn’t mean you’re failing.

Taking a little longer doesn’t mean you’re losing.

And not having everything figured out doesn’t mean you’re behind.

Sometimes, progress looks very small.

Getting out of bed and trying again.

Finishing one assignment.

Praying even when you don’t feel like praying.

Opening your Bible after being away from it for a while.

Choosing to keep going.

Those small steps matter.

Keep Walking

I don’t know what your journey looks like right now.

But if you’re feeling behind, remember this:

You don’t have to finish everything today.

Just take the next step.

Trust God with the things you cannot control, and be faithful with what He has placed in front of you today.

Your journey is still being written.

So don’t give up just because you’re not where you want to be yet.

Keep walking.

God isn’t finished with you.

“For a just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again.” — Proverbs 24:16

Maybe you’re not behind.

Maybe you’re simply still on your way.
