I Don't Know What Comes Next

Ashley Janer September 22, 2026

One of the hardest things about growing up is not knowing what comes next.

College makes you think about the future a lot.

What career will I have?

Where will I work?

Will my plans work out?

Will I make the right decisions?

Sometimes, I wish I could see the whole picture before making a single move.

But life doesn’t work that way. I Want to Know the Plan

I’ll be honest—I like knowing what’s going to happen.

I want to know if the decision I’m making is right.

I want to know if the things I’m working hard for will eventually pay off.

I want to know where I’ll be a few years from now.

But God doesn’t always show us the entire plan.

Sometimes, He gives us just enough direction for today.

“Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding.” — Proverbs 3:5

Trusting God means accepting that I won’t always understand what He’s doing.

My Plans Are Not Always His Plans

I have my own dreams.

I have things I want to achieve and places I want to go.

There’s nothing wrong with having goals.

But I’ve learned that I shouldn’t hold my plans so tightly that I forget to leave room for God’s direction.

Proverbs 16:9 says:

“A man’s heart deviseth his way: but the LORD directeth his steps.”

I can make plans.

I can work hard.

I can prepare for my future.

But I also need to be willing to let God change my direction.

What If Things Don’t Go as Planned?

Maybe the course I take won’t lead exactly where I expected.

Maybe a door I wanted will close.

Maybe something I’ve prayed for won’t happen.

Maybe I’ll have to start over.

Those possibilities can be scary.

But I don’t want fear of the unknown to stop me from moving forward.

A closed door doesn’t automatically mean the end of the journey.

Sometimes, it can simply mean there’s another direction.

I may not understand it immediately.

That’s where faith comes in.

Learning to Trust

I’m still learning how to trust God with my future.

It’s not always easy.

There are days when I worry.

There are days when I overthink.

There are days when I want answers right away.

But I’m learning to pray instead of constantly trying to control everything.

Instead of asking only:

“God, what is going to happen?”

I want to learn to ask:

“God, how can I honor You with where I am today?”

That changes the way I look at the future.

I Don’t Need to Know Everything

Maybe I don’t need to know what comes next.

Maybe I just need to know who I’m walking with.

The future is still unknown to me, but it isn’t unknown to God.

So I’ll keep studying.

I’ll keep working.

I’ll keep dreaming.

I’ll keep praying.

And I’ll keep taking the next step.

“Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established.” — Proverbs 16:3

I don’t know exactly where this road will take me.

But I know I don’t have to walk it alone.

I may not know what comes next, but I know Who holds my future.
