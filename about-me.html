About Me

Ashley Janer September 22, 2026

Hi, I'm the person behind College Days With God. 🤍

I'm a college student learning to balance school, dreams, responsibilities, and my walk with God.

I don't have everything figured out. I'm still learning, growing, making mistakes, and discovering where God is leading me.

Why I Created This Blog

I created College Days With God as a place to share my experiences, thoughts, Bible reflections, and lessons I've learned along the way.

College isn't just about grades and deadlines. It's also a season of discovering who we are, facing challenges, making decisions, and growing in faith.

I want God to be part of that journey.

What You'll Find Here

🌿 My Walk — personal stories and lessons from my journey.

📖 Faith — Bible verses, devotionals, and reflections.

🎓 College Life — school, struggles, goals, and everyday experiences.

💭 Reflections — thoughts about life, growth, and the lessons God teaches me.

“Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding.”

I'm still becoming, and I'm still walking.

One day. One lesson. One prayer. One step at a time.
