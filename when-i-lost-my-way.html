When I Lost My Way

Ashley Janer September 22, 2026

There are seasons in life when you don't exactly know where you're going.

You wake up, go to school, finish your responsibilities, talk to people, scroll through your phone, and continue with your normal routine.

Everything looks okay from the outside.

But inside, something feels different.

You feel tired.

You feel distant.

You don't feel as close to God as you used to.

And sometimes, you don't even know when it started.

I Didn't Notice It at First

I didn't wake up one day and decide to stop spending time with God.

It happened slowly.

One busy day became another.

I became distracted.

I started telling myself that I would pray later or read my Bible when I had more time.

But "later" kept getting pushed back.

Eventually, I realized that I was trying to handle everything on my own.

Sometimes We Get Lost Quietly

Getting lost doesn't always look dramatic.

Sometimes it looks like losing motivation.

Sometimes it looks like constantly worrying about the future.

Sometimes it looks like being surrounded by people but still feeling alone.

Sometimes it looks like knowing what the Bible says but struggling to actually live it.

And sometimes, it's simply realizing that you've been walking for a while without looking toward God.

Finding My Way Back

The good thing about being lost is that you can still turn around.

I don't have to pretend that everything is okay.

I can come to God honestly.

I can tell Him that I'm tired.

I can tell Him that I'm confused.

I can tell Him that I've made mistakes.

I can simply pray:

"Lord, help me find my way back to You."

And I believe He hears that prayer.

Coming Back Doesn't Have to Be Perfect

Sometimes we think we need to fix ourselves before coming back to God.

We think we need to pray better.

Read more.

Become more disciplined.

Stop making mistakes.

But maybe the first step is simply coming back.

Open the Bible.

Say a prayer.

Sit quietly.

Start again.

Even if it's only for a few minutes.

One Step Back

I've learned that my walk with God isn't about never getting lost.

There will be moments when I become distracted.

There will be seasons when my faith feels weak.

There will be days when I don't know what I'm doing.

But I don't want those moments to be the reason I stop walking.

I want them to remind me to look back toward God.

“Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you.” — James 4:8

Maybe you're in that kind of season right now.

Maybe you've been distant from God.

Maybe you've been carrying something quietly.

Maybe you don't know where your life is going.

You don't have to figure everything out today.

Just take one step.

Then another.

And keep walking.

Sometimes finding your way doesn't mean discovering a new road.

Sometimes it means turning back to the One who was there all along.
