When God Seems Silent

Ashley Janer September 22, 2026

Have you ever prayed about something again and again, but nothing seems to change?

You pray for an answer.

You pray for a problem to go away.

You pray for something you’ve been waiting for.

But days pass.

Then weeks.

Sometimes, even months.

And you start wondering:

“Is God listening?”

I’ve had moments like that too.

Silence Doesn’t Mean Absence

When we don’t receive the answer we were hoping for, it’s easy to think that God isn’t listening.

But silence doesn’t mean God has left.

There are things God is doing that we cannot see yet.

Isaiah 55:8-9 reminds us:

“For my thoughts are not your thoughts, neither are your ways my ways, saith the LORD.”

God sees things differently than we do.

We see what is happening right now.

God sees the whole picture.

Sometimes the Answer Is “Wait”

Waiting is hard.

Especially when we want an answer immediately.

As college students, we’re used to deadlines and schedules. We want to know when something will happen and what the result will be.

But God doesn’t always work according to our timeline.

Sometimes He says yes.

Sometimes He says no.

And sometimes He says wait.

Waiting doesn’t mean doing nothing.

It can be a season of learning, growing, praying, and trusting.

Psalm 27:14 says:

“Wait on the LORD: be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine heart: wait, I say, on the LORD.”

What If I Don’t Understand?

There are prayers I still don’t understand the answer to.

There are things I wish had happened differently.

There are moments when I ask God why something had to happen in the first place.

And maybe I won’t always get the explanation I want.

But faith isn’t always about having every answer.

Sometimes, faith is choosing to trust God even when I don’t understand.

I can say:

“Lord, I don’t understand this, but I still trust You.”

That’s not weakness.

That’s faith.

Keep Praying

If you’ve been praying for something for a long time, don’t give up just because you haven’t seen an answer yet.

Keep talking to God.

Tell Him what you’re afraid of.

Tell Him what you’re hoping for.

Tell Him when you’re disappointed.

Prayer doesn’t require perfect words.

God already knows what’s in our hearts.

And while we’re waiting for an answer, we can ask Him to help us become stronger, wiser, and closer to Him.

Maybe the Waiting Is Part of the Journey

Looking back, some of the things I desperately wanted didn’t happen the way I expected.

At the time, I didn’t understand why.

But sometimes, what felt like a closed door became a different direction.

What felt like a delay became a lesson.

What felt like silence became a season where I learned to trust God more.

I still don’t understand everything.

And that’s okay.

I don’t need to know everything to keep walking.

I just need to trust the One who does.

“Trust in him at all times; ye people, pour out your heart before him: God is a refuge for us.” — Psalm 62:8

If you’re waiting for an answer today, keep praying.

Keep trusting.

Keep walking.

God is not limited by the silence you can hear.
