Finding Time for God in a Busy College Life

Ashley Janer September 22, 2026

College can get really busy.

There are assignments to finish, projects to work on, exams to study for, and responsibilities that seem to keep coming.

Sometimes, my schedule gets so full that I realize I’ve gone through the whole day without spending time with God.

I tell myself, “I’ll pray later.”

Then later becomes tomorrow.

And tomorrow becomes another day.

Before I know it, I’ve already been putting God at the bottom of my list.

When Life Gets Busy

I think one of the hardest things about college is learning how to manage everything.

We make time for school.

We make time for friends.

We make time for social media.

We make time for the things we enjoy.

But sometimes, we struggle to make time for the One who gave us another day in the first place.

It’s not always because we don’t care about God.

Sometimes, we’re simply distracted.

Sometimes, we’re tired.

Sometimes, we think we need to finish everything else first.

But God shouldn’t only be part of our schedule when we have free time.

It Doesn’t Have to Be Complicated

Spending time with God doesn’t always have to mean having a long Bible study or spending an hour in prayer.

Sometimes, it can start with something simple.

A short prayer before class.

Reading a few verses before going to sleep.

Thanking God for something good that happened during the day.

Asking for wisdom before making a decision.

Taking a few minutes to be quiet and remember that God is with us.

Small moments can become meaningful when we consistently give them to God.

Psalm 119:105 says:

“Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.”

I don’t need to know the whole path.

I just need enough light for the next step.

Put God First

Matthew 6:33 says:

“But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.”

Putting God first doesn’t mean ignoring school or responsibilities.

It means remembering who comes first in my life while doing those things.

I can study hard and still pray.

I can work toward my goals and still trust God.

I can enjoy college life while remembering that my identity isn’t based on my grades, achievements, or what other people think about me.

Start Again

Maybe you’ve also been distant from God lately.

Maybe you haven’t opened your Bible in weeks.

Maybe your prayer life isn’t what it used to be.

Don’t let guilt convince you that you can’t start again.

Just start.

Open your Bible.

Talk to God.

Even if the prayer is short.

Even if you don’t know what to say.

Come as you are.

There’s no need to wait until your life is perfectly organized before you make room for God.

Start with today.

One verse.

One prayer.

One quiet moment.

Then do it again tomorrow.

College may be busy, but I don’t want to become so busy building my future that I forget the God who holds my future.

“Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established.” — Proverbs 16:3

So today, before I rush into everything else, I want to remember Him first.
